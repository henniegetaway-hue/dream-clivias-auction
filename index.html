Dream Clivias Auction — Rules & Terms

By registering as a buyer or seller, you agree to these rules.

Buyer Rules

1. Buyers must register with correct contact details and full delivery address.

2. A valid bank account number must be supplied and confirmed before bidding is allowed.

3. Bids may increase by R50 or R100 at a time.

4. Higher bidding limits require the applicable qualification fee before bidding above that level.

5. Qualification fees are paid once for each higher bracket reached.

6. Winning bidders must pay their invoice within the required period.

7. Unpaid invoices may result in warnings. After the 5th warning, the buyer may be banned and must register again from the beginning.

Seller Rules

8. Sellers must register with correct contact details and a valid bank account number.

9. Seller bank details must be confirmed before selling/payment functions are enabled.

10. The seller listing fee is R50 per plant / auction spot.

11. If a plant sells for more than R2,000, the seller pays Dream Clivias a commission equal to 10% of the winning bid, less the R50 listing fee already paid. No commission applies at R2,000 or below.

12. Sellers are responsible for sending sold plants to the winning buyer.

Plant Photo Rules

13. No business logos, phone numbers, WhatsApp numbers, websites, email addresses or advertising may appear on plant photos.

14. Breeding names and a small copyright mark are allowed on plant photos.

15. Flowers containing more than 20% orange colour are automatically rejected.

Export Services

16. Export service provider details are not displayed publicly.

17. A R500 export connection fee applies before export-service contact details are released.

Payments & Privacy

18. Payment information may be checked against registered bank account details and payment amounts.

19. Buyer delivery/contact details are shared only where required to complete a winning order.

20. Dream Clivias Auction may update these rules when necessary. Updated rules may require acceptance again.
